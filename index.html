Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What areas of Sydney do you service?

We supply and install turf right across Greater Sydney — the Hills District, Northern Beaches, Sutherland Shire, Western Sydney, and out to the Blue Mountains and Wollongong. Not sure if your suburb is covered? Call us and we'll confirm straight away.

How much does turf cost?

It depends on the area size, the variety you choose, how much ground preparation is needed and site access. Book a consultation and we'll give you a clear, itemised quote based on your actual job — not a one-size-fits-all estimate.

How soon can I get my turf?

Because we cut to order, most turf is ready within a day or two of booking. Have your site prepped before your delivery date — fresh turf should be laid as soon as possible after harvesting so it establishes quickly and evenly.

Which turf variety is right for my property?

It comes down to sun and shade, foot traffic, budget and how much maintenance you want to do. That's exactly what the free consultation is for — we'll walk you through the differences and recommend the best fit for your site.

Can you prepare the ground as well as lay the turf?

Yes. Our installation service includes removing the old grass, levelling the ground and laying the correct underlay, so your new turf goes onto a properly prepared base rather than over unsuitable soil.

How do I look after new turf?

Water deeply once or twice a day for the first two to three weeks while it roots, then taper back to two or three times a week. First mow is usually two to three weeks in, on a high setting. We supply full aftercare guidance with every order.
