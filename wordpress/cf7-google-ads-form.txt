WHY IT CHANGED FROM YOURS
-------------------------
1. No `id:` attributes.
   This one form is output twice on the page (hero + contact). Duplicate ids
   mean a <label for="hf-n"> in the contact form focuses the HERO field, so
   clicking a label scrolls the user back up the page. Wrapping each control
   inside its own <label> associates them implicitly and needs no ids, so the
   form is safe to reuse.

2. The submit is wrapped in <div class="cf7-submit">.
   CF7 renders the submit as an <input>, and an <input> renders no ::before
   and cannot contain the arrow span. Left with class:btn class:btn-primary
   it would inherit that style's WHITE background and WHITE text and the
   label would be invisible. The wrapper is the button instead: it carries
   the fill, the glow, the hover retract and the arrow.

3. Dropped the <p class="hf-note"> line.
   The template already prints that note under the form from the ACF field,
   so leaving it here shows it twice.

4. Added the fields the design has: suburb is now required, plus approx.
   area, turf variety and what they need.
